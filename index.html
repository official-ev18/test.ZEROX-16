About the studio

Built in
the dark

The Zerox Game Studios is an independent team focused on telling stories through mood, texture, and silence. We believe the best games feel like a place you have to find your way through.

We design, illustrate, and build everything in-house, so every cable, every shadow, and every sound stays true to the vision.
