About the game

SilentGears is an atmospheric exploration game set in a world of old machinery, tangled wires and dead trees. Nothing here is explained directly. Every gear, cable and abandoned mechanism hints at a story the player has to piece together.

The game is built around slow discovery. There is no rush, no timer and no map marker. The goal is to notice details, follow strange sounds and understand the world through its systems.

  • GenreAtmospheric exploration
  • PerspectiveFirst person
